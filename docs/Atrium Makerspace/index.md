# Makerspace Overview

**Location: [SHED A350](https://maps.rit.edu/?details=Atrium+Makerspace){target="_blank"}**

The Atrium Makerspace is home to the makerspace's more advanced equipment. This includes industrial 3D printers, 2 metal shops, a wood shop, a paint booth, and a large open workspace.

Specific policies related to the Atrium makerspace can be found [here](./Atrium%20Makerspace%20Policies.md).

The following equipment can be found in the Atrium Makerspace:

## Central Makerspace Equipment

* [Advanced 3D Print Farm](./Advanced%203D%20Printing.md)
* Hand tools, such as drills, screwdrivers, wrenches, hot glue guns, bike pump, etc.
