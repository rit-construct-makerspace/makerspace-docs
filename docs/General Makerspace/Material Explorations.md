# Material Explorations

The Materials Explorations Space, or MatX for short, is a space where makers can get hands-on with a wide range of materials, finishes, and manufacturing processes to understand what choices are best for your project. MatX goes beyond quantitative comparisons of materials; over 1000 materials are available to hold and interact with, so you get a feel for your project will look and feel.

For more information, please contact make@rit.edu
