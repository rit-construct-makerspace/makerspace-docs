# Welding Info

**American Welding Society Club: SHED Welding open hours. Tues: 5-8pm**

AWS is available to help Capstone (and other) students in welding tasks and projects. 

It is best practice to email welding@rit.edu with the details/pictures/drawings of parts and assemblies before coming to open hours. This allows us to save the students time with any pre-work that we would need completed before we can help. 

We can weld carbon steel, stainless steel and aluminum. Our primary processes are MIG and TIG.

*Note: We do no provide material! Please bring your own.*
